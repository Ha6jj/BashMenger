All commands:
  help - open this page
  h - open this page
  q - close the app
  i - open chat page
  ip <ip to connect> - add new ip to your client. Example: 127.0.0.1
  port <port to connect> - add new port to your client. Example: 8080
  room <room name to connect> - add new room to your client. Example: cppPractise
  pass <password to connect with> - add new password to your client. Example: 6Hel7llo
  connect - imitate connection to chat-room with your port, ip, room and pass
  disconnect - imitate leaving a chat-room
  prefs - open Pref page
  p - open Pref page
press ESC to leave
