All commands:
  help - open this page
  h - open this page
  q - close the app
  i - open chat page
  ip <ip to connect> - add new ip to your client. Example: 127.0.0.1
  port <port to connect> - add new port to your client. Example: 8080
  room <room name to connect> - add new room to your client. Example: cppPractise
  name <login to connect with> - add new login to your client. Example: Sasha
  pass <password to connect with> - add new password to your client. Example: 6Hel7llo
  connect - connects to the server with your port and ip
  register - makes new account on server with your name and password
  auth - enters the account with your name and password
  join - enters the chat-room with your room name
  leave - leaves the chat-room with your room name
  disconnect - disconnects from the server with your ip and port
  delete - deletes chat-room with your room name
  create - creates a new room with your room name
  prefs - open Pref page
  p - open Pref page

[most commands use presaved name, password, ip, port and room name]
press ESC to leave
